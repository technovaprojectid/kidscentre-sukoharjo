About Kids Centre Sukoharjo

Kids Centre Sukoharjo is an educational institution dedicated to supporting children's growth from early education to academic achievement. With a philosophy of Learning is Fun and Enjoyable, we create a warm, professional, and child-friendly learning environment.

We currently run two flagship programs under one institution:

  • Kids Centre AHE — Spesialis CaLisTung-Ji (Baca, Tulis, Hitung, Mengaji) untuk anak usia 0–12 tahun.
  • MEA Edu — Bimbingan akademik, English, Mathematics, dan Private Class untuk siswa SD, SMP, dan SMA.

Both programs share the same commitment: helping every child learn joyfully and reach their full potential.
